Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think, learn, and solve problems. The main goal of AI is to develop systems that can perform tasks which normally require human intelligence. These tasks include recognizing speech, understanding language, making decisions, and even playing games. AI can be classified into two types: narrow AI, which is designed for specific tasks, and general AI, which has the ability to perform any intellectual task that a human can do. With rapid advancements in computing power and data availability, AI is becoming more accessible and useful in everyday life.

2. Machine Learning
Machine Learning is a subset of AI that enables computers to learn from data without being explicitly programmed. It involves training algorithms to identify patterns and make predictions or decisions. For example, a machine learning model can analyze thousands of images of cats and dogs, and then correctly classify a new image as either a cat or a dog. Popular machine learning techniques include supervised learning, unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning. These techniques are widely used in applications such as email filtering, speech recognition, and personalized marketing.

3. Natural Language Processing (NLP)
Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a field of AI that focuses on the interaction between computers and human languages. NLP allows machines to read, understand, and respond to text or voice inputs in a way that feels natural to humans. Common applications of NLP include chatbots, virtual assistants like Siri or Alexa, language translation, and sentiment analysis. NLP combines computational linguistics with machine learning and deep learning to enable machines to understand context, tone, and intent in communication. This technology plays a crucial role in customer service automation and content moderation.
